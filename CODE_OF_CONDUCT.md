# Code of Conduct

EWS welcomes technical disagreement, critique, and competing designs. Participation must remain professional and focused on the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful toward contributors and reviewers.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Provide enough technical context for claims and proposed changes.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, spam, and deliberate disruption.
- Respect privacy and never expose another person's confidential data or credentials.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove abusive or unsafe participation and may restrict access when necessary to protect contributors or the project.
